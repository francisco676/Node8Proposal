Questions buyers actually ask

What you're probably wondering. Plain answers.

I get it's important. But why not wait a quarter?+
Audience is the only marketing asset that compounds. Every month you wait is a month it can't. Founders who delay a quarter typically restart from cold: the followers earned in a spike post drift away, the algorithm forgets them, and the next viral hit takes longer to surface than the first one did. Your own data shows it: one 335K post in month one, then five months under 36 reactions. That's the audience the algorithm already stopped showing you to. Waiting another quarter doesn't pause the curve; it pushes the restart cost up.
Why not just have ChatGPT or Claude do this?+
You can. Most LinkedIn content already does. Originality.ai's 2025 study of 3,368 posts from 99 influential profiles flagged 53.7% of long-form LinkedIn content as likely AI-generated. That's exactly the problem. The reason posts hitting your feed feel forgettable is they all read the same. We use AI heavily for the unsexy work (scraping competitor posts, tracking what category leaders publish, keeping your context graph fresh), and real humans on our team write every post. The system gives us category fluency on day one; the humans give you something the algorithm hasn't already seen 10,000 times this week.
My buyers (CEOs, CFOs, COOs) don't really post on LinkedIn. Do they actually read it?+
They read it. CEOs are heavy LinkedIn readers and light posters. They scan the feed before board meetings, forward posts to chiefs of staff, and DM founders whose content they've been quietly reading for weeks. The lower the post-to-read ratio in your ICP, the more weight each post carries when it lands. Three on-voice posts a week, in front of the right CEOs, runs closer to a sales motion than a content one. That's the channel your community is already running on. Your customers told you so on the call ("they see my posts on WhatsApp"). LinkedIn is the same surface, with more reach.
What if I don't want to post, or hate the drafts?+
Nothing ships without your approval. Five-minute Slack review every cycle. If a draft misses, we rewrite that day. If the system isn't clicking by day 30, we pause, re-scope, and you keep every post, the voice node, and the context graph regardless of what we decide next. No clawback fights, no "we just need more time."
How are you different from generic founder-content agencies?+
Most agencies sell engagement metrics. We measure ICP-relevant audience growth. Most agencies write generic operator-life posts in the voice you'd expect. We start from a Context Graph specific to your category, backstory, and the angles Node8 already owns. The cadence is the product, not the volume.
What happens after 90 days?+
If it's working, we keep going (month-to-month, same rate, still no auto-renewal). If it isn't, we shake hands and you keep every post, the voice node, the context graph, and the playbook for running it in-house. The 90 days are designed so the renew/walk call gets made on real data, not vibes.
